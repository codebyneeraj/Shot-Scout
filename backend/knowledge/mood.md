# Mood to shots

## Loneliness and isolation
tags: lonely, alone, isolation, empty, solitude, melancholic

- Wide or extreme wide with the character small in the frame and lots of empty space (negative space).
- Static camera, long held shots; place the character off-centre.
- Pull-out to end the scene; tracking from behind as they walk away.

## Tension and suspense
tags: tense, tension, suspense, fear, threat, danger, waiting

- Tight close-ups and ECUs on eyes, hands, objects that matter.
- Slow push-ins that build; hold on the listener, not the speaker.
- Low-key light, hard shadows; cut tighter as the scene progresses.
- Keep information off-screen: frame doors, corners, the dark.

## Intimacy and love
tags: intimate, love, romance, tender, close, kiss

- Long lenses (85–135mm) with shallow focus, soft light, close-ups.
- Two-shots that keep both in frame; over-the-shoulder that is "dirty" (shoulder visible).
- Slow gimbal or static; avoid jittery handheld.

## Grief and sadness
tags: grief, sad, sorrow, loss, funeral, bittersweet, crying

- Soft overcast or low, warm light; muted palette.
- Hold on faces longer than comfortable; let silence play.
- Wide shots that show the empty place where someone was.

## Joy and energy
tags: joy, happy, celebration, party, dance, fun, energetic

- Wider lenses close to the action, handheld or gimbal, movement through the space.
- High-key, bright light; quicker cutting; slow motion (48–60 fps) for peak moments.

## Chaos and action
tags: chase, fight, run, action, chaos, panic, escape

- Handheld or gimbal, tracking alongside; wide lenses close to the action.
- Many short angles; inserts on hands, feet and objects; whip-pans to link.
- Higher shutter speed (1/100+) for crisp, staccato motion.

## Power and confrontation
tags: confrontation, argument, power, interrogation, boss, threat

- Low angle on the dominant character, high angle on the weaker one.
- Shift the angles as the power shifts in the scene.
- Singles get tighter as the argument escalates; break the 180° line only on purpose.

## Mystery and discovery
tags: mystery, discover, reveal, search, investigation, secret

- Reveal through movement: push past a foreground object, crane up, pull-out.
- POV shots for discovery, then the reaction close-up.
- Low-key light with a single motivated source.
