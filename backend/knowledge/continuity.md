# Continuity and craft rules

## 180 and 30 degree rules
tags: core

- 180° rule: keep the camera on one side of the line between two characters so screen direction stays consistent. Cross it only on purpose (with a moving shot or a neutral shot).
- 30° rule: change the angle by at least 30° (or the size clearly) between consecutive shots of the same subject to avoid a jump cut.

## Eyelines and screen direction
tags: dialogue, conversation, chase, walk, travel

- Characters talking to each other must look in opposite directions across cuts (one left of lens, one right).
- A journey keeps one screen direction (e.g. always left to right) unless the story turns around.

## Matching across a long day
tags: exterior, day, golden_hour, overcast

- Light changes; shoot all shots of one direction together.
- Note the sun position per shot so pickups can match.

# Style presets

## Noir
tags: noir, crime, detective, night, low_key

- Low-key, hard light, deep shadows, practicals, venetian-blind patterns, wet streets, low angles, wide lenses close to faces.

## Handheld documentary
tags: documentary, realism, handheld, verite

- Handheld, 28–35mm, natural light, reframing as things happen, long takes.

## Symmetrical / formal
tags: symmetrical, formal, comedy, whimsical

- Centered compositions, static or straight lateral tracking, wide lenses, flat front light, bold palette.

## Epic landscape
tags: epic, landscape, journey, nature, mountain, desert

- Extreme wides with the character tiny, golden hour or blue hour, crane or drone, long lens to stack mountains.

## Intimate drama
tags: drama, family, intimate, realism

- Close-ups on long lenses, soft window light, quiet static frames, minimal movement.
