# Vocabulary

Every section starts with a `tags:` line. `core` sections are sent with every request;
the rest are picked by the retriever when their tags match the scene.

## Allowed shot values
tags: core

Use ONLY these values in the output. Anything else is rejected.

- **size**: EWS (extreme wide), WS (wide), MWS (medium wide), MS (medium), MCU (medium close-up), CU (close-up), ECU (extreme close-up), INSERT, OTS (over the shoulder), POV, TWO-SHOT
- **lens_mm**: 14, 18, 24, 28, 35, 50, 85, 100, 135
- **movement**: static, pan, tilt, push-in, pull-out, tracking, handheld, gimbal, crane, dolly-zoom, whip-pan
- **light_plan**: natural, backlit, silhouette, side-light, soft-overcast, low-key, high-key, practical, motivated-window, negative-fill, bounce-fill

## What lenses do
tags: core

- 14–24mm: shows space and environment, exaggerates depth and distance, distorts faces up close. Use for establishing, isolation, scale, energy in tight spaces.
- 28–35mm: natural, "documentary" field of view. Good for walk-and-talk, handheld, medium shots that keep context.
- 50mm: close to how we see; neutral, honest. Good for mediums and singles that should not feel stylised.
- 85–135mm: compresses background, flatters faces, separates subject from background with shallow focus. Use for close-ups, intimacy, surveillance/voyeur feeling, stacking crowds or traffic.
- Longer lens = more compression and shallower depth of field at the same framing.

## What movement means
tags: core

- static: observation, stability, formality, lets the performance carry the shot.
- push-in: growing realisation, rising tension, intimacy. Slow push = internal shift.
- pull-out: revelation of context, loneliness, ending.
- tracking: following a journey; from behind = we follow them into the unknown; leading in front = we watch their reaction.
- handheld: urgency, instability, documentary truth. Avoid for calm scenes.
- gimbal: smooth, dreamy, floating; good for long walks and continuous takes.
- crane: scale, opening or closing a sequence, god's-eye.
- dolly-zoom: disorientation, sudden realisation; use at most once per film.
- whip-pan: energy, comedy, linking two spaces.
